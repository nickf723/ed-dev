# Architecture remaster contract

## Ontology boundary

Architecture keeps eight direct branches, in registry order:

1. Architectural Design & Studio
2. Building Technology & Construction
3. Architectural Structures
4. Environmental & Building Systems
5. Urban Design & Site Planning
6. Architectural History & Theory
7. Representation & Digital Fabrication
8. Professional Practice, Codes & Accessibility

All eight remain visible and non-clickable until substantive pages exist. The root presents them as coordinated fields, not a required sequence. Materials, sustainability, interiors, landscape, preservation, housing, computation, and narrower building types should first be placed within or across these branches rather than added as root peers without an ontology review.

## Root lesson flow

1. Choose a direct architectural field from the section atlas.
2. Move between body, room, building, site, and city scales.
3. Use firmness, utility, and delight as a historical question set rather than a universal rubric.
4. Trace how changes propagate across space, structure, environment, construction, and people.
5. Calculate plan area, drawing scale, and a simplified ramp ratio.
6. Resolve a coordination conflict and read the standards boundary.

## Model contract

`architectureModel.ts` owns:

- exact eight-branch parity
- rectangular area arithmetic
- drawing-millimetre to actual-metre scale conversion
- rise-to-run calculation
- four deterministic assessment cases and correctness

The page may visualize these relationships differently, but no component should maintain a second arithmetic truth table.

## Representation boundary

A plan, section, elevation, diagram, model, simulation, and completed building are related representations with different evidence limits.

- plans support horizontal arrangement, adjacency, and dimensions
- sections support vertical relationships, level changes, envelopes, and assemblies
- scale drawings permit exact conversion only when the scale and measurement are known
- coordination overlays reveal conflicts but do not solve them automatically
- diagrams and teaching arithmetic do not certify structural, environmental, accessibility, life-safety, or code compliance

## Standards boundary

The 1:12 assessment calculates run from rise for a simplified stated ratio. It is not a complete ramp design. Real accessibility review can also require landings, clear width, handrails, cross slope, rise limits, edge protection, surface conditions, egress coordination, adopted standards, and local requirements.

Energy, structural, fire, and accessibility requirements depend on jurisdiction, project type, adopted edition, and authority having jurisdiction. The page must link to official starting points while telling learners that professional application requires the complete applicable sources and qualified coordination.

## Vocabulary contract

Architecture owns its root vocabulary at `applied.architecture`. The registry-derived scope inherits upward into Applied Science. Narrower terms can move to direct branches when those routes become substantive.

## Visual contract

The root world is a deterministic architectural sheet: a real schematic section, room labels, stairs, a courtyard void, site contours, a plan inset, dimensions, load cues, and fixed daylight rays. It must remain spatial and sectional rather than inheriting Music's temporal mixer or Chemistry's specimen bench.

The root background must not depend on random values, viewport measurement, canvas drawing, animation loops, or a post-hydration first frame. Major surfaces keep open intervals so the section remains legible as scenery.
