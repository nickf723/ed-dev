# Sociology remaster contract

## Scope and ontology

Sociology keeps seven direct planned branches: Social Interaction; Groups & Networks; Institutions; Stratification & Inequality; Population & Demography; Social Change; and Theory & Methods. Micro, meso, macro, and cross-cutting bands are analytical scale cues, not false curriculum parents or a ranking. Direct branches remain inert until their pages exist.

## Learning flow and model

The root proceeds from branch navigation and a cross-scale trace through the preserved Schelling-style emergence lab, three theoretical lenses, a four-case evidence review, and public-data boundaries. The initial 20×20 grid is generated from seed 64 with 54 vacancies, 182 group-A agents, and 164 group-B agents. At a 30% threshold it begins with 81 dissatisfied agents, 77% satisfied, and 48% mean local similarity. Seeded steps preserve group counts and vacancies while making every fixture reproducible.

The model demonstrates a possible local-rule-to-aggregate-pattern mechanism. It does not measure attitudes or establish a single cause of residential segregation. Law, discrimination, wealth, markets, zoning, institutions, history, and geography remain explicit boundaries.

## Vocabulary and assessment

Twenty-nine terms have one narrowest current owner: fifteen at Sociology and two within each direct branch. Stable IDs for Anomie, Ethnocentrism, and Social Stratification remain intact. Assessment distinguishes model from world, counts from rates, aggregate from individual inference, and a coded network edge from universal friendship or influence.

## Visual, data, and hydration contract

A deterministic server-rendered micro→meso→macro field replaces the random viewport canvas. The page returns to server ownership; only the seeded simulation and assessment are client islands. The Census API, available-dataset index, BLS Public Data API, and data.census.gov are future repository protocols. Results must retain dataset, universe, variable, category, estimate, denominator, unit, geography, period, release, revision, suppression, uncertainty, series or table identity, and source. No provider fetch occurs during render.

## Verification

The checkpoint must pass targeted React/Next lint, TypeScript, deterministic model tests, vocabulary aggregation, all three informational audits, a production build, generated-HTML checks, exact remote-tree parity, and a zero-deployment guard before publication to `studio`. Rendered responsive and interaction checks remain queued when the browser runner is unavailable.
