# Physics remaster contract

## Ontology

Physics keeps eight direct registry-owned routes: Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Waves & Optics, Relativity, Quantum Physics, Atomic Physics, and Nuclear Physics. Familiar-scale and extended/specialized banks are model-regime cues, not false curriculum parents. Every active route remains clickable and in registry order.

## Learning flow

The root proceeds from direct navigation through a reusable define → measure → model → test cycle, an eight-scenario regime instrument, a one-segment measurement bench, a finite exact-constant reference, and a four-case assessment. The regime instrument routes from physical conditions rather than prestige or difficulty; companion routes acknowledge systems that cross fields.

## Measurement and model truth

The motion bench keeps origin, endpoint, elapsed time, direction, unit, displacement, distance, velocity, and speed synchronized. The assessment checks signed average velocity, SI conversion, uncertainty-aware agreement, and patterned-residual diagnosis. A successful prediction constrains a model but does not uniquely prove it, while a discrepancy triggers bounded checks before a claim is expanded.

## Vocabulary ownership

Thirty-five terms have one narrowest current owner: thirteen at Physics and twenty-two across Mechanics, Motion, Kinematics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Waves & Optics, Relativity, Quantum Physics, Atomic Physics, and Nuclear Physics. Existing IDs for Entropy, Quantum Entanglement, Kinematics, Position, Displacement, Velocity, Acceleration, and Speed remain stable. All descendant terms aggregate through Physics into Natural Science.

## Reference collection boundary

The root displays four exact revised-SI defining values: the speed of light, Planck constant, elementary charge, and Boltzmann constant. It does not imply that every fundamental constant is exact. The NIST constants reference remains version-aware for measured CODATA values, uncertainty, and correlations. A future Atomic Spectra Database adapter must retain species, ionization stage, units, wavelength medium, observed or Ritz status, uncertainty, output fields, version, and bibliography; the page makes no render-time provider request.

## Visual and hydration contract

The root uses a deterministic server-rendered measurement field: fixed trajectory samples, a velocity vector, a field response, a wave trace, coordinate grids, and a unit ruler. It has no random values, canvas, viewport reads, timers, animation effects, or wall-clock state. Client ownership is confined to the regime selector, measurement bench, and evidence review, each with deterministic initial state and accessible controls.
