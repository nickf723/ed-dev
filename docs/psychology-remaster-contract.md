# Psychology remaster contract

## Ontology boundary

Psychology keeps six direct branches, in registry order:

1. Cognitive Psychology
2. Biological Psychology
3. Developmental Psychology
4. Social & Personality Psychology
5. Clinical & Counseling Psychology
6. Methods & Measurement

The branches are compatible lenses rather than mutually exclusive partitions or a required sequence. Methods and measurement operate across all five substantive perspectives. Health, educational, industrial-organizational, environmental, forensic, sport, comparative, and other specialties should first be placed within or across this bounded layer rather than added as root peers without an ontology review.

All six direct destinations remain non-clickable until each route becomes substantive. The root must orient the field without pretending that a branch placeholder is a lesson.

## Root learning flow

1. Choose a direct lens from the observation register.
2. Learn that one behavior can support biological, cognitive, developmental, social, personality, and contextual explanations at once.
3. Manipulate signal, competition, rehearsal, and interference in the preserved cognition laboratory.
4. Connect a construct to an explicit operational definition.
5. Separate a numerical difference from the causal claim a design can support.
6. Distinguish educational models, research, and professional clinical practice.

## Model contract

`psychologyModel.ts` owns:

- exact six-branch parity;
- the fixed uninterrupted and interrupted score sets;
- mean and condition-difference arithmetic;
- four deterministic evidence cases and answer verification.

The first assessment file is rote and exactly calculable. The remaining files transfer the same evidence discipline to operationalization, self-selection, causality, and the clinical boundary.

## Evidence boundary

A psychological construct is not identical to one task, survey response, score, biological measure, or visualized model. Operational definitions make a construct testable under stated conditions; reliability, validity, sampling, uncertainty, and converging measures govern the interpretation.

An observed group difference or correlation can motivate explanation without isolating a cause. Random assignment addresses some preexisting differences, but no single design eliminates every measurement, sampling, implementation, or generalization question.

All participant identifiers, conditions, scores, paths, and numerical outputs on the page are invented teaching fixtures. They are not records of real people or empirical estimates of human performance.

## Research-ethics and clinical boundary

The root links to HHS human-subject guidance and teaches respect for persons, beneficence, and justice as starting principles rather than a complete review protocol. Real research must follow the appropriate law, institutional review, consent, privacy, risk, and participant-protection requirements for its setting.

The cognition laboratory and evidence review are educational. They do not screen, assess, diagnose, predict, or recommend treatment for a person. Personal mental-health concerns belong with an appropriate qualified professional or local support service. The page links to NIMH help resources without turning the route into clinical advice.

## Vocabulary contract

Psychology owns its root vocabulary at `social.psychology`. The registry-derived scope inherits upward into Social Science. Narrower terms can move to direct branches when those routes become substantive.

## Visual and hydration contract

The root world is a deterministic observation room: masked participant IDs, fixed condition bars, observed-versus-construct notes, trial markers, and inference boundaries. It must feel behavioral and evidentiary rather than architectural, anatomical, or like a decorative brain network.

The root background does not depend on random values, viewport measurement, canvas drawing, animation loops, or a post-hydration first frame. Rendered reaction time begins only after hydration and remains in a ref until a learner completes a trial, so wall-clock time cannot alter the initial server/client tree.
