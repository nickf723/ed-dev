# Philosophy remaster contract

## Ontology

Philosophy keeps seven direct registry-owned branches: Metaphysics, Epistemology, Philosophy of Mind, Ethics, Political Philosophy, Aesthetics, and Philosophy of Science. Metaphysics, Ethics, and Aesthetics are active; the other four remain visible and inert. The question matrix is a map of overlapping emphasis, not a rigid coordinate taxonomy or a replacement hierarchy. Logic remains a Formal Science cross-link rather than a Philosophy child.

## Learning flow

Direct branch navigation appears immediately after the header. The root then clarifies the practice of philosophy, maps one epistemology argument into question, claim, reasons, objection, and reply, distinguishes the role of formal logic, and tests four reasoning cases. Positions are not presented as teams or as lists of famous names; the lesson emphasizes questions, concepts, reasons, targets, and revision.

## Assessment

The four-case review distinguishes validity from soundness, a counterexample from a proof of the universal opposite, necessary from sufficient conditions, and an argument-level objection from a personal or field-level rejection. One tested model owns the canonical argument tree, exact branch parity, and all answer paths.

## Vocabulary ownership

Twenty-eight terms have one narrowest current owner: twelve at Philosophy and sixteen across Metaphysics, Epistemology, Philosophy of Mind, Ethics, Normative Ethics, Consequentialism, Political Philosophy, Aesthetics, and Philosophy of Science. Existing IDs for Epistemology, Solipsism, Utilitarianism, and Determinism remain stable. All descendant vocabulary aggregates through Philosophy into Humanities.

## Source and repository boundary

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy is linked as an expert-edited changing reference; its fixed archives are linked separately for stable citation. The page links rather than redistributing entry text. Crossref's REST API is reserved for bibliographic metadata and DOI trails, not philosophical claims or full-text rights. A future adapter retains author, title, work type, container, publisher, dates, DOI, relation, license, correction/retraction context, edition, and source link while leaving argument modeling to curated site records.

## Visual and hydration contract

The dialectic field is a deterministic server-rendered set of fixed threads and junctions. It has no client directive, random values, viewport reads, effects, timers, animation loops, or wall-clock state. Client ownership is confined to the question matrix, argument inspector, and evidence review. Required argument text stays at or above the site's 11px readability floor.
