# Site Analysis & Constraints Page Plan

## Identity

**Route:** `/applied-science/architecture/site-context/analysis-constraints`  
**Parent:** Site & Context  
**Page type:** Atomic lesson

## Learning contract

**Learner goal:** distinguish mapped facts from later design interpretations and responses.

**Primary mental model:** a site-analysis drawing is a stack of evidence layers. Conditions should be located, sourced, and verified before they are labeled opportunities or constraints.

**Evidence of understanding:** the learner can select a site condition and separate what is actually known from questions, sources to verify, and possible architectural responses.

## Scope

Belongs here:

- study/property boundaries as spatial references
- terrain, water, vegetation, utilities, access, surrounding context, and regulatory research as evidence categories
- uncertainty and source verification
- fact → question → verification → possible response

Does not belong here:

- real legal advice or parcel-specific zoning claims
- climate optimization
- detailed grading/drainage design
- final site planning
- a goodness score for a site

## Primary instrument

A hypothetical layered site drawing with individually toggleable evidence layers and a condition ledger.

Selecting a condition should reveal:

1. mapped fact
2. question raised by the fact
3. information/source needed to verify it
4. one possible design response

The instrument must never imply that hiding a layer makes that condition irrelevant.

## Guardrails

- clearly identify the example site as hypothetical
- do not present illustrative review bands as actual setbacks/easements
- legal constraints require project- and jurisdiction-specific sources
- unresolved information may remain explicitly uncertain
- property boundaries do not bound all relevant context

## Visual verification

Check that:

- all evidence layers are visually distinguishable
- selected-condition highlighting remains readable
- toggling a layer does not resize the instrument
- the ledger never clips its longest text
- the map still reads as evidence, not a fake GIS application
- the hypothetical-site disclaimer is visible but subordinate
