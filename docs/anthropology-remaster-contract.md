# Anthropology remaster contract

## Educational job

The Anthropology root is a four-field navigation hub and evidence-boundary
lesson. It helps a learner choose among cultural, biological, archaeological,
and linguistic evidence; combine fields around one human question; preserve the
context of a record; calculate one bounded observation share; and treat ethics,
consent, stewardship, and consultation as part of knowledge production.

It is not a catalog of cultures, a ladder of human evolution, a fixed racial
typology, a display case for decontextualized objects, or a substitute for the
active Archaeology branch.

## Registry and navigation contract

`ANTHROPOLOGY_CURRICULUM` owns exactly four direct fields in this order:

1. Cultural Anthropology — planned
2. Biological Anthropology — planned
3. Archaeology — active
4. Linguistic Anthropology — planned

The four evidence streams are peers, not stages or containers for whole groups
of people. The page derives labels, descriptions, status, hrefs, and
breadcrumbs from the registry and fails loudly when model order drifts. The
active route links; planned routes remain visible and inert.

## Lesson flow

1. Four-field route atlas and evidence compass
2. Motion-free hominin branching viewer beside one meal examined four ways
3. Observe → compare → contextualize → collaborate method rail
4. Four-file evidence review
5. Ethics and collection-source boundary shelf

Major sections use 96px scenery corridors so the static field-record world can
remain visible between foreground surfaces.

## Shared model and exact practice

`anthropologyModel.ts` owns direct branch parity, five bounded hominin teaching
specimens, observation-share arithmetic, deterministic percent formatting, and
four evidence answers.

The exact practice fixture is:

`18 observed shared-preparation events / 48 recorded events = 37.5%`

That result is exact for the stated log, not automatically for a community,
culture, population, time period, motive, or value. Counts, sampling frame,
interpretation, generalization, and ethical consequence remain distinct.

The evidence review also tests:

- observation versus ethnographic interpretation;
- overlapping human variation versus fixed biological race typology;
- object description versus archaeological provenience, association,
  stewardship, and legal/ethical permission.

## Vocabulary ownership

Anthropology owns 10 broad terms. Each direct field owns two specialized terms,
for 18 unique terms in the subtree:

- `anth-ethnography` and `anth-cultural-relativism` belong to Cultural
  Anthropology;
- `anth-bipedalism` and biocultural approach belong to Biological
  Anthropology;
- provenience and stratigraphy belong to Archaeology;
- speech community and language ideology belong to Linguistic Anthropology.

Stable legacy IDs remain unique, definitions are revised, and the complete
scope aggregates into Social Science with branch provenance preserved.

## Visual and hydration contract

The root background is a deterministic server-rendered SVG field record. It
coordinates observation notes, a speech event, an evidence-bounded claim,
stratigraphy, provenience, human-variation overlap, and an
observe–record–contextualize–consult chain. It uses no canvas, viewport reads,
random values, timers, animation frame, or hydration-time drawing.

The hominin viewer initializes to a fixed specimen and changes through a native
range input without Framer Motion. The evidence review initializes from fixed
model data. Required interface copy stays at least 11px, focus is visible, and
motion is not necessary to understand the route.

## Ethics and repository boundaries

The page links to official sources and performs no render-time fetch:

- [AAA anthropological ethics](https://americananthro.org/about/anthropological-ethics/)
  for professional responsibilities around harm, honesty, consent, competing
  obligations, access, records, and relationships;
- [Smithsonian Open Access developer tools](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/devtools)
  for collection metadata, API and weekly JSON access, and rights-aware media;
- [National NAGPRA databases](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nagpra/databases.htm)
  for compliance-document abstractions involving inventories, summaries,
  notices, grants, and other repatriation work.

A future material-culture adapter must retain provider and unit, stable record
ID, title, people/culture fields as source assertions, dates, places,
description, collection and credit, rights/media status, source URL, refresh
context, and uncertainty. It must support restricted/sensitive/no-media states
and explicit records that should not be displayed. Searchability, CC0 metadata,
or absence from a compliance table is never treated as consent, endorsement,
legal possession, ethical permission, or freedom from consultation.
