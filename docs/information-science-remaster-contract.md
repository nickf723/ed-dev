# Information Science remaster contract

## Scope

This checkpoint covers the Information Science root and its one active direct descendant, Taxonomy & Ontology. It preserves the existing entropy and vector-retrieval instruments while bringing their math, curriculum hierarchy, vocabulary ownership, assessment, background, and source boundaries under one contract.

## Ontology

- The root owns seven direct fields: Information Theory & Coding, Encoding & Representation, Information Retrieval & Search, Taxonomy & Ontology, Archives & Digital Preservation, Information Behavior & Interaction, and Bibliometrics & Science of Science.
- Metadata & Semantics is a specialty inside Encoding & Representation.
- Knowledge Graphs & Linked Data is a specialty inside Taxonomy & Ontology.
- The record inspector must derive indentation from those registry relationships. It may not present a decorative hierarchy that differs from the sidebar and vocabulary tree.
- Taxonomy & Ontology remains the only active child route in this family. Knowledge Graphs is visible but inert until its own page exists.

## Learning flow

The root proceeds from direct navigation to empirical entropy, synthetic vector retrieval, a provenance-aware collection protocol, information-system questions, a four-file claim assessment, and official standards. The child proceeds from its direct specialization to controlled-vocabulary/taxonomy/ontology distinctions, one shared fictional game represented as a browse hierarchy and typed graph, governance, deterministic practice, and standards.

## Models and boundaries

- Entropy is computed from observed single-symbol frequencies and is never described as meaning or exact compressed file size.
- Retrieval ranks fixed records by Euclidean distance in a synthetic two-dimensional teaching space. Distance does not independently establish truth, utility, fairness, or universal relevance.
- The Library of Congress connector is a no-key, rate-limited, public API blueprint. The page links a live bounded query but performs no build-time or render-time request.
- API results are query slices shaped by endpoint coverage, cataloging, digitization, facets, pagination, and item-level rights statements.
- The taxonomy/ontology comparison uses one fictional board game so the representation can change while the described object remains fixed.
- A taxonomy is not falsely limited to a strict single-parent tree; polyhierarchy, mappings, and local policy remain visible.
- An ontology is not reduced to a decorative network or a generic claim that relations are “good for AI.”

## Vocabulary

Fifty-eight terms have one narrowest curriculum owner across the root and its branches. Established IDs for Information, Metadata, Classification, Taxonomy, Information Retrieval, Index, and Entropy remain stable. Parent drawers aggregate descendant vocabulary through curriculum containment.

## Visual and hydration contract

The root is an information-flow desk: source marks pass through representation gates into described records and a query path. The child is a classification atelier: broader/narrower paths and typed relation graphs occupy opposite margins. Both backgrounds are deterministic server-rendered SVG/CSS. The removed canvas backgrounds may not return through viewport reads, random tree generation, or animation loops.

## Source boundary

Primary references are the Library of Congress JSON/YAML API, PREMIS, and METS documentation plus the W3C RDF 1.1, SKOS, and OWL 2 recommendations. The interface paraphrases these sources and links them directly; it does not claim local conformance merely because it teaches their concepts.
