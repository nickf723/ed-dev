# Earth Science remaster contract

## Ontology boundary

Earth Science keeps five direct branches, in registry order:

1. Geology
2. Hydrology
3. Meteorology
4. Physical Geography
5. Climatology

Mineralogy is an active reference child of Geology, not a sixth root peer. Its existing route and curated specimen cabinet remain unchanged; the root previews it inside the Geology band. Physical Geography owns landforms, surface processes, terrain, and environmental spatial patterns. Human Geography remains a cross-link to Social Science rather than a second Earth Science child.

Climatology remains visibly planned. It crosses atmosphere, ocean, ice, land, and life through long-term distributions, forcing, variability, and feedback, so the root must not depict it as another physical shell stacked above or below the other fields.

## Root learning flow

1. Choose one of five direct Earth Science fields from the ridge-to-coast transect.
2. See Mineralogy contained at the material scale within Geology.
3. Follow weathering, water and energy, tectonic relief, and climate feedback across field boundaries.
4. Close one simplified watershed budget while keeping inputs, outputs, storage, interval, and boundary visible.
5. Distinguish weather from climate and local observations from global or exclusive-source claims.
6. Inspect official observation repositories and the model-versus-measurement boundary.

## Model contract

`earthScienceModel.ts` owns:

- exact five-branch root parity;
- the Mineralogy child identity;
- the fixed 120 / 45 / 55 mm teaching water budget;
- `ΔS = precipitation − evapotranspiration − runoff` arithmetic;
- four deterministic evidence cases and answer verification.

The budget does not imply that those are the only real watershed fluxes or reservoirs. Components, signs, units, spatial boundaries, and time intervals must be stated before combining values.

## Evidence boundary

Earth-system evidence is scale-bound. A station, hand specimen, stream sample, map cell, satellite pixel, core layer, model run, and regional average measure different objects through different procedures. Local change can be valid without supporting a global rate, single mechanism, or universal classification.

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions and events. Climate describes statistical patterns over long periods for defined places, variables, and comparison periods. Individual weather observations contribute to the climate record but do not determine its long-term distribution alone.

## Collection and source boundary

The root links to NASA Earth-system research, USGS water pools and fluxes, and NOAA NCEI as official observation and archive starting points. It does not fetch or present provider measurements in this pass. Every transect mark, station identifier, score, flux bar, and budget value on the route is an invented teaching fixture.

Mineralogy remains the specialized curated Earth-material collection. A future live Earth observation catalog must normalize a typed provider response, preserve dataset and variable provenance, expose time and spatial coverage, state resolution and units, and distinguish no-result, loading, cached, partial, and provider-failure states.

## Vocabulary contract

Earth Science owns its broad systems vocabulary at `natural.earth-science`. Mineralogy owns specimen-specific terms at `natural.earth-science.mineralogy`; after the ontology repair those terms aggregate through Geology into Earth Science and Natural Science without hand-copying.

## Visual and hydration contract

The root world is a deterministic field transect from ridge to channel to coast: atmosphere, cloud and rain, snow, topography, stream, groundwater, ocean, strata, fault cues, and fixed monitoring stations. It must feel like a place being observed across scales rather than a generic glowing globe.

The root background does not depend on random values, viewport measurement, canvas drawing, animation loops, pointer hover, or a post-hydration first frame. Client ownership is limited to the flux controls and evidence review; curriculum, navigation, background, sources, and first render remain server-owned.
