# Education remaster contract

## Ontology

Education keeps nine direct registry-owned branches: Learning Sciences; Curriculum & Instruction; Assessment & Measurement; Instructional Design; Accessibility & Special Education; Educational Technology; Teaching & Learning Environments; Education Policy & Systems; and Teacher Learning & Professional Practice. All nine remain visible and inert until their own routes exist. The three folios are overlapping conceptual work areas, not false curriculum parents, a sequence, or a hierarchy.

Psychology and Technology remain cross-disciplinary neighbors rather than Education children. The root does not reduce education to classroom technique: it coordinates learner, content, instruction, evidence, access, environment, profession, institution, and system.

## Learning flow

Direct branch navigation appears immediately after the header. The root then uses an alignment studio to compare one learning goal with a practice opportunity and evidence task, presents experience → practice → feedback → transfer as one revisable planning loop rather than a universal stage theory, and ends with a four-case evidence conference plus source shelf.

The alignment model distinguishes direct, partial, and weak sampling. Those labels describe compatibility with one toy target; they do not rank teaching methods, forecast learning, or turn a learner's performance into an enduring trait.

## Assessment

The four-case conference distinguishes a related selected response from direct evidence of causal explanation, a score or praise from actionable feedback, score reliability from a validity argument, and an incidental interface demand from the construct being assessed. One tested model owns all goal/activity/evidence matrices, next-move rules, folio coverage, branch parity, and answer paths.

## Vocabulary ownership

Twenty-eight terms have one narrowest current owner: ten at Education and two within each of the nine direct branches. The root owns the concepts needed across the field—goal, prior knowledge, instruction, practice, feedback, learning evidence, transfer, opportunity to learn, scaffold, and alignment. Specialized measurement, curriculum, accessibility, technology, environment, policy, learning-science, design, and professional-practice terms remain with their branches and aggregate into Applied Science through curriculum containment.

## Source and repository boundary

The What Works Clearinghouse is linked as a repository of practice guides, intervention reports, study reviews, and downloadable review data; a review is not an endorsement or universal prescription. NCES Common Core of Data is linked for documented U.S. public-school and district records; every future record must retain year, geography, universe, unit, release status, missingness, and revision notes, and descriptive data may not be presented as a causal estimate or learner diagnosis.

CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 is linked as a revisable design reference rather than a guarantee or individualized determination. The official U.S. Department of Education IDEA source is linked for current statute, regulations, and implementation material; jurisdiction, eligibility, age, setting, current guidance, and individual circumstances remain attached, and the page does not provide legal advice. The root performs no render-time provider fetch.

## Visual and hydration contract

The learning-evidence wall is a deterministic server-rendered sequence of attempts, feedback, revision, transfer, changing concept connections, and evidence sources. It has no client directive, random values, viewport measurement, effects, timers, wall-clock state, animation loop, or hydration-only scenery. Client ownership is confined to the alignment studio and evidence conference. Required learner-facing text remains at or above the site's 11px readability floor.

## Verification

The checkpoint must pass targeted React/Next lint, TypeScript, deterministic education-model tests, vocabulary aggregation, the three informational audits, a production build, generated-HTML checks for branch parity, planned non-links, default alignment, assessment, source boundaries, metadata, and static background, plus a zero-deployment guard before publication to `studio`. Responsive rendering and interaction checks remain queued when a trusted browser runner is unavailable.
